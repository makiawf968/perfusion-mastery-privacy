📋 Overview

Perfusion Mastery ("the App") is developed and maintained by MAK™. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the App on iOS and Android devices.

This app is for educational purposes only and is not affiliated with EBCP, AmSECT, APS, or any other certification body.
Free access depends only on having an account. Refusing or withdrawing survey consent, and leaving the profession field blank, change nothing about what you can use.

📊 Information We Collect

We collect the following information:

  • Account information: Your email address, used solely to create and manage your account via Firebase Authentication.
  • Usage data: Quiz results, session history, and progress statistics — stored locally on your device and optionally synced to Firebase.
  • Purchase information: Subscription status managed by RevenueCat. We do not store your payment details; these are handled by Apple's App Store or Google Play.
  • Device information: Basic technical information (operating system version, device model, IP address) is processed by Firebase and RevenueCat as part of signing you in and managing a subscription. The App itself runs no crash-reporting or analytics tool, and we do not build a profile of your device.
  • Profession (optional): If you choose to state it when registering, we store a professional category chosen from a fixed list — never free text, never your employer, never your workplace. You may leave it blank, and the account behaves exactly the same.
  • Survey consent: Whether you agreed to be contacted about the app, and the date you agreed. It is off unless you turn it on, and you can turn it off again at any time in the app's settings.

🔒 How We Use Your Information

Your information is used exclusively to:

  • Authenticate your account and maintain your session
  • Save and display your learning progress
  • Manage your subscription and unlock premium content
  • Improve the app's content quality, from the aggregate analysis described below and from feedback you choose to send us
  • Write to you about the service itself — an outage, a change to these terms, a significant new version. This is part of providing the account and does not depend on survey consent.
  • Contact you about surveys or product feedback — only if you turned that on, and never for third-party marketing.
  • Analyse quiz results in aggregate (by chapter and by professional category) to find weak areas in the question bank. This analysis carries no identifier that points back to you, and its results are never published in a form that could single out one person.
We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

🗄️ Data Storage

Your progress data is stored locally on your device. If you are signed in, quiz sessions and progress may be synced to Firebase (Google) servers located in the European Union or the United States, subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

  • Firebase Authentication — Google Privacy Policy
  • RevenueCat — RevenueCat Privacy Policy
  • Apple App Store — Apple Privacy Policy
  • Google Play — Google Privacy Policy

⚖️ Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

We process your personal data on the following legal grounds under the EU General Data Protection Regulation:

  • Contract (Art. 6.1.b GDPR) — to provide your account, subscription, and progress tracking.
  • Legitimate Interest (Art. 6.1.f GDPR) — to keep the service secure and working, and to improve the question bank.
  • Consent (Art. 6.1.a GDPR) — for optional cloud synchronization of your progress, for the optional profession field, and for being contacted about surveys.
Consent must be freely given (Art. 7.4 GDPR). None of the three consents above is a condition of using the App or of free access, and withdrawing any of them costs you nothing.

🗓️ Data Retention

We retain personal data only as long as needed for the purposes described above:

  • Account data: retained while the account is active + 30 days after deletion.
  • Quiz progress / session history: 24 months maximum.
  • Profession and survey consent: retained while the account is active, and erased with the account. A withdrawn consent is kept only as the fact that it was withdrawn and when, so that we can prove we stopped.
  • Subscription receipts: as long as required by Apple/Google + 7 years for accounting (legal obligation in France/Switzerland).

🌍 International Data Transfers

Some of our processors operate infrastructure outside the European Economic Area:

  • Firebase and RevenueCat process data on Google/AWS infrastructure (US).
  • Transfers are covered by Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) under EU Commission decision 2021/914.

👤 Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your data
  • Delete your account at any time via the app settings or by contacting us
  • Withdraw your survey consent at any time, from the App's settings — no email needed, and your access is unaffected
  • Withdraw consent to cloud synchronization at any time; your progress then stays on your device only
