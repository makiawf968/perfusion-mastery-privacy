📋 Overview

You can permanently delete your Perfusion Mastery account and the personal data associated with it at any time. There are two ways to do this:

  • Directly from inside the app (recommended, fastest).
  • By emailing the developer if you no longer have access to the app.
Account deletion is permanent. Your progress, statistics, and subscription data linked to your account cannot be restored after deletion.

📱 Delete From Inside the App

Follow these steps on your iOS or Android device:

  1. Open Perfusion Mastery.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Scroll down to Danger Zone.
  4. Tap Delete Account.
  5. Confirm your choice when prompted.

Your account will be removed immediately and a confirmation will appear on screen.

📬 Delete by Email (Fallback)

If you can no longer access the app, you can request deletion by email:

  • To: makiawf968@gmail.com
  • Subject: Account deletion request
  • Body: include the email address used to sign up so we can locate your account.

We will process your request within 30 days and confirm by reply.

🗑️ What Gets Deleted

When you delete your account, the following data is removed:

  • Firebase Auth credentials — your authentication record.
  • Firestore progress document — quiz results, session history, and statistics linked to your account.
  • Firestore profile document — your professional category and your survey consent.
  • RevenueCat customer data — the link between your account and your subscription is severed.
  • Local app data — cached content and progress stored on your device.

🗓️ Retention of Backups

Encrypted backups maintained by our infrastructure providers (Firebase / Google Cloud) may retain residual copies of your data for up to 30 additional days after deletion, after which the data is permanently erased.

Subscription receipts may be retained longer where required by Apple/Google policies or by tax and accounting law (see the Privacy Policy for details).
